# Community code of conduct

Everyone is welcome regardless of background, identity, nationality, experience, or hardware access. Be patient with beginners and people communicating in another language.

- Criticize ideas with evidence. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks are not acceptable.
- Respect privacy. Do not share others' personal information, private messages, credentials, or data without permission.
- Stay relevant. Do not spam, pressure people to donate, advertise returns, or misrepresent affiliations and test results.
- Credit others, disclose material AI assistance, and correct factual mistakes.

The maintainer may request changes, remove harmful content, restrict participation, or escalate abuse to GitHub. Where practical, explain actions and allow a response; safety/privacy concerns may need immediate action.

Flag visible moderation concerns for the maintainer without repeating sensitive details. GitHub's **Report content / Report abuse** options provide a private route to GitHub for harassment, threats, or privacy violations. No separate project conduct mailbox or independent panel exists yet. Reports about the maintainer should go to GitHub rather than that maintainer. Do not publish private evidence in issues.
