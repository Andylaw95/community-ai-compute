# Building the community

**Can voluntary compute sharing make useful AI more accessible while preserving participants' control, safety, and trust?**

“For humanity” is our mission, not a measured-impact or nonprofit-status claim. It means welcoming people across backgrounds and budgets, listening to those who need compute, and valuing evidence over promotion.

## Participation

- [Discussions](https://github.com/Andylaw95/community-ai-compute/discussions): introductions, use cases, questions, alternatives, and design proposals.
- [Issues](https://github.com/Andylaw95/community-ai-compute/issues): bugs, scoped experiments, and tasks with acceptance criteria.
- Pull requests: focused improvements linked to an issue where appropriate.
- [Private security reports](SECURITY.md): exploitable findings awaiting assessment/fix.

Introduce your interests, what you tried, and what you might enjoy contributing. Participation is voluntary. Hardware, money, contact details, and public identity beyond a GitHub account are not prerequisites for discussion.

## Decisions and stewardship

[Andylaw95](https://github.com/Andylaw95) is the initial repository maintainer. There is no foundation, elected board, or established contributor team yet. The maintainer handles settings and merges; technical proposals are open for public review.

Record significant decisions with problem, options, evidence, tradeoffs, and conclusion in a discussion or PR. Link maintainer decisions to that reasoning and leave unresolved questions visible. Proposals are not delivery promises. Adding maintainers should follow sustained contributions, a public proposal, and agreement on responsibilities.

## Proposed first development cycle

1. **Reproduce:** collect platform reports and independent correctness fixtures.
2. **Listen:** collect real use cases, including education and public-interest work; select one with permissions and acceptance criteria.
3. **Measure:** compare against a single-machine baseline, including verification, transfer, retries, and resource impact.
4. **Decide:** publish results and choose integration, a missing component, further work, or stopping an approach.

Dates depend on volunteers and agreed scope. Remote execution, GPU sharing, sensitive data, and money need separately defined boundaries.

Healthy progress means reproducible results, useful feedback, reviewed contributions, and documented decisions. Stars and node counts alone do not demonstrate impact.
